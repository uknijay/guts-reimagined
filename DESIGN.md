# GUTS website design system

The site should feel like a welcoming student-made event poster wall, not a corporate tech product. Its visual cues come from GUTS's existing Instagram artwork: saturated university blue, cyan highlights, hexagonal geometry, high-impact display lettering, and event-specific accent colours. The source event posters and committee portraits carry the character; surrounding UI should stay clear and restrained.

- **Palette:** cobalt `#0752d1`, deep blue `#123887`, dark blue `#102f77`, sky `#81d8fc`, paper `#f8fbff`, yellow accent `#ffdf68`. Use yellow sparingly for focus, calls to action, and the duck.
- **Type:** Oxanium Bold for display, DM Sans for reading and UI. Both are locally hosted OFL fonts. GUTS's existing Supermolot files are trial/personal-use and must not be shipped until the society supplies a licensed webfont.
- **Layout:** dense, poster-like hero; open editorial rhythm afterward. Events show the real artwork rather than recreating it as generic cards. Committee portraits use the brand's hexagonal framing.
- **Interaction:** clear links and buttons, visible keyboard focus, mobile navigation, reduced-motion support. The duck is a small optional surprise, never a barrier to content.
- **Content:** facts and names come from the repository content files. Historical events are explicitly presented as an archive; never suggest those dates are upcoming.
