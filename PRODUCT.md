# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

University of Glasgow students discovering GUTS, finding events and community, and deciding whether to join. Prospective partners also have a dedicated route.

## Product Purpose

Present Glasgow University Tech Society and its events, people, and partnership opportunities. Give visitors a clear route to the society's Discord and email contact.

## Operating Context

The site is a student society homepage with a partners page. Events and committee profiles are edited through JSON content files and a Decap CMS configuration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js 16 and React 19 application.
- Preserve working home, partners, external join, email, and social routes.
- Event and committee content is sourced from `public/content/events.json` and `public/content/site.json`.
- Historic events in the content file should be presented as past work, not advertised as upcoming.
- Do not invent sponsors, attendance counts, testimonials, or future event dates.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the recognisable Glasgow University Tech Society identity and real logo.
- Follow the GUTS Drive branding guidelines and the Instagram references supplied in the request: royal/electric blues, cool event colours, hexagons and circuitry, confident technical lettering, and discreet ducks.
- Keep the site welcoming, playful, and student-made while reaching a professional quality standard.
- The Drive's Supermolot files are trial fonts with a personal-use licence; a public website needs a licensed copy before self-hosting them.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing logo, event poster images, and committee portraits in `public/assets/`.
- Real event descriptions and team data in `public/content/`.
- Brand guidelines: https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1oLH_56OPF66gOtOWzqm3VPC0TpGfXtpBbQOqVU1Naic/edit
- Instagram grid screenshots supplied by the user in this task.

## Product Principles

- Put the people and actual event work ahead of abstract technology decoration.
- Make events and joining easy to find on every screen size.
- Keep event identities distinct inside a recognisable parent GUTS system.
- Treat factual and dated content carefully.
